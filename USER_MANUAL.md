# TheConstructor - User Manual

This is a quick guide on how to use the website once it is running. If the site is not running yet, check README.md first to set it up.



# Making an account

The first time you use the site you need to sign up.

1. Click the Get Free Estimate button on the home page.
2. Switch to the Sign Up tab.
3. Type your email and a password. The password needs at least 6 characters.
4. Click Sign Up.

After that you are logged in automatically. Next time you come back you only need to log in with the same email and password.



# Uploading a blueprint

Once you are logged in you will see the dashboard.

1. Click Upload Blueprint.
2. Pick a floor plan image from your computer. JPG and PNG both work fine.
3. Wait a few seconds. The AI will scan the image and find the walls, doors and windows.

You will see the detected stuff highlighted on top of your image. If it missed something or got something wrong you can edit it manually.



# Fixing the scale

The AI does not know how big your drawing is in real life so you have to tell it.

1. Find a wall in the drawing where you know the actual length in meters.
2. Click that wall.
3. Type the real length, like 5 for 5 meters.
4. Click Set Scale.

Now all the other measurements will be correct.



# Filling the form

On the right side there is a form. Fill it in like this.

Soil type. Pick what kind of ground the building is on. Soft clay needs more concrete than hard rock so this matters.

Wall thickness. Usually 9 inches for normal houses.

Cement brand. Pick whichever brand you plan to buy. The price changes a bit depending on the brand.

Wastage percentage. Usually 10. This is for the extra material you waste when cutting and mixing.

Plinth height and wall height. The plinth is the part of the wall under the floor and the wall height is from the floor to the ceiling. 0.45 and 3.0 meters are the normal values.

Number of floors and floor area. Self explanatory.

Door count and window count. The AI fills these in automatically but you can fix them if the count is wrong.



# Getting the estimate

Click the Calculate button at the bottom of the form.

After a second or two you will see the bill of quantities. It tells you how many cement bags, how much sand, how much steel and how much rubble you need. Everything is shown in LKR.



# Saving the estimate

Click Download PDF to save the estimate as a PDF file. You can print it or send it to a client.



# Logging out

Click the Logout button on the top right of the dashboard. That clears your login and takes you back to the home page.



# If something goes wrong

If the AI does not detect anything, the image might be too blurry or too small. Try a clearer one.

If Calculate does nothing, check that you set the scale and filled in the form. The button needs all the fields.

If the page just shows a white screen, check the two terminal windows from the README. One of them probably has a red error and you need to restart it.

That is pretty much it. Have fun using the site.
